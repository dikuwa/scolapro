# Conduct, Achievement and Learner Support

## Purpose

ScolaPro must maintain a balanced longitudinal picture of the learner. Conduct records must not become a database of punishment only, and sensitive support information must not be broadly exposed.

## Separate Domains, Connected Timeline

The learner timeline may contain:

- conduct incidents
- positive achievements/commendations
- referrals
- counselling/support interventions
- accommodations
- health/support observations where authorized
- follow-up actions
- parent/guardian communication

These records share a learner timeline but use different permissions, workflows and retention rules.

## Conduct Incident Model

A conduct incident should capture:

- learner
- date/time
- category and reason
- description
- location/context
- staff reporter
- severity/points only if school policy uses them
- learners/staff involved where permitted
- evidence/attachments
- immediate action
- referral/escalation
- resolution
- linked communication
- audit history

Multiple incidents on different days are independent events; users should never need to overload one record with multiple reasons.

## Reason Registry

Use searchable categories rather than one enormous dropdown. Example groups:

- Attendance-related
- Academic conduct
- Behaviour/interpersonal
- Property/resources
- Safety
- Digital conduct
- Other school-defined categories

School-configured points and disciplinary consequences must be policy data, not hard-coded application logic.

## Positive Achievement

Support positive events such as:

- academic improvement
- leadership
- helpfulness/service
- sporting/cultural achievement
- excellent work
- attendance improvement
- school-defined merits

Achievements can feed certificates, learner profiles, awards and CRC outputs where applicable.

## Learner Support

Learner-support records can include:

- referral source
- concern/category
- initial observation
- assigned responsible person
- intervention/support plan
- accommodations
- meetings
- follow-up dates
- outcome/status
- restricted notes/documents

Sensitive categories such as health, disability, pregnancy, psychological, domestic violence and vulnerability require stricter field-level or record-level permissions than ordinary conduct records.

## Privacy Rules

- Ordinary teachers see only what is necessary for teaching/support.
- Counsellor/learner-support roles can access authorized restricted records.
- HOD/principal visibility follows policy and necessity, not blanket access.
- Regional/Ministry analytics should normally receive aggregates/de-identified indicators rather than unrestricted case notes.
- Every sensitive-record access should be auditable where practical.

## CRC Integration

CRC rendering consumes approved longitudinal facts from academic, attendance, achievement and authorized learner-support domains. The CRC document is a view/output, not the primary database.

When the official CRC format becomes available, required fields map onto this foundation without rewriting historical records.
