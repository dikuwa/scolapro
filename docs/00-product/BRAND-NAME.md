# ScolaPro brand naming contract

The canonical product brand name is **ScolaPro**.

## Required usage

Use exactly `ScolaPro` in all user-facing product naming, including:

- application shell and navigation;
- login/authentication pages;
- browser/page metadata;
- emails and notifications generated by the platform;
- exported system documents where the platform itself is credited;
- help/support copy;
- public/marketing pages;
- developer-facing product documentation where the product name is referenced.

## Do not use as product names

The following are not approved product-brand spellings and should not be introduced into UI copy:

- `Schola`
- `Schola Pro`
- `SchoolPro`
- `School Pro`
- `ScholarPro`
- `Scholar Pro`
- `Scola Pro`

The repository/package slug may remain lowercase `scolapro` where technical identifiers require lowercase/no-space naming.

## School identity vs platform identity

A tenant school keeps its own legal/display identity. School names, logos, contact details and school-specific document typography must never be replaced by the ScolaPro brand.

For example, Namib High School may use its own configured blackletter/Old English school-name treatment on its official report cards. That treatment is school-scoped and must not become a ScolaPro platform font or a default for other schools.

## Implementation rule

Prefer shared brand constants/components instead of repeatedly hardcoding the product name. New AI/coding agents must preserve this naming contract unless an explicit product rebrand is approved.
